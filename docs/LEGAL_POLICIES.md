# Public policy pages

The web application includes public routes:

- `/privacy`
- `/terms`
- `/acceptable-use`
- `/abuse-report`

The policy copy describes current Repus behavior, including consent records, unsubscribe suppression, approximate engagement tracking, development-only simulated delivery, SES delivery when configured, and platform enforcement. It avoids claiming that the product itself determines whether a workspace has legal permission to send.

## Before production publication

The deployment operator must complete and publish its legal identity and contact details, privacy-request channel, hosting regions and subprocessors, retention schedule, effective dates, governing law, commercial terms, and applicable regional rights and complaint process. The current pages explicitly disclose these deployment-specific gaps; they are not a substitute for legal review. The product does not yet provide a self-service account deletion or privacy-request workflow.
